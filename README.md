<h2>Project Overview</h2>
<p>Commissioned by a local school employee, this project follows the complete data analytics lifecycle, from data cleaning and preparation to exploratory modeling and visualization.</p>

<h3>Key Areas of Investigation:</h3>
<ul>
 <li><strong>Teacher Experience & Impact:</strong> Examining instructor backgrounds—specifically analyzing teacher years of experience (ranging up to 30 years)—and how educator tenure correlates with student test score outcomes.</li>
  <li><strong>Test Score Progression:</strong> Evaluating longitudinal score patterns and growth trajectories across successive student assessment periods.</li>
  <li><strong>Skill Mastery & Course Grades:</strong> Tracking student progress across specific academic standards and course evaluations to pinpoint achievement milestones.</li>
</ul>
